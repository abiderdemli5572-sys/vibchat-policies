Privacy Policy

VibChat does not collect, store, or share any personal data without the user’s consent.
All messages, media, and account information are securely processed through Firebase services.

Information We Collect

Phone number or email for account creation

Profile photo (optional)

Messages, photos, videos, and voice notes sent by the user

Crash logs (for improving performance)


How We Use Your Data

To enable real-time messaging

To secure your account

To improve app performance

To prevent spam, abuse, and harmful content


Data Sharing

VibChat never shares your data with third parties.
Data is only processed through Firebase (Google), a secure and trusted infrastructure.


---

Child Safety Policy (CSAM & CSAE Protection)

VibChat is committed to protecting minors and strictly prohibits:

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)

Child sexual exploitation (CSAE)

Grooming or any harmful communication toward minors


Protection Measures

Automatic detection & blocking of harmful content

Reporting and moderation tools

Immediate removal of illegal content

Account banning for violators

Cooperation with legal authorities if required
